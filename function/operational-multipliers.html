Operational Multipliers

function
Operational Multipliers are not message creators—they are force expanders.
Author
Affiliation

E. Rosalie Li

Information Epidemiology Lab

Published

February 24, 2026

Operational Multipliers are not message creators—they expand the reach and impact of that which already exists in an operation–the people, the message, the harm. They increase the scale, persistence, and perceived legitimacy of influence operations without altering the core narrative. They do not produce propaganda but make it louder, more persistent, and harder to counter. These mechanisms act across digital, institutional, and commercial environments to simulate popularity, engineer credibility, and embed influence into the informational terrain.

Unlike Legitimation, which draws power from social or institutional prestige, or Obfuscation, which hides the origin of influence, Operational Multipliers extend the effects of influence horizontally and vertically. They make a narrative feel urgent, ubiquitous, and endorsed—not through trust in the source, but through repetition, reach, and engineered resonance.

These tools are modular, reusable, and often deniable. Many are third-party actors, including commercial entities, freelance influencers, criminal enablers, and loosely aligned civil groups. Some are automated, others organic but coordinated. Their power is not in the argument, but in the architecture. They are designed to alter the informational environment itself.

Operational Multipliers are actors and mechanisms that amplify the impact of influence operations without producing the original message. Their role is not to create content but to increase its visibility, credibility, and persistence, making propaganda appear popular, trustworthy, and widely accepted. These tools operate across digital, institutional, and commercial environments, enhancing the reach and durability of core narratives.

Key types of operational multipliers include:

  • Commercial Malign Influence: Private firms and digital mercenaries who offer information manipulation as a service, commodifying influence operations and enabling deniability.
  • Data Grooming and Poisoning: Strategic seeding of biased or false content into AI training datasets to subtly shape future machine outputs and limit epistemic boundaries.
  • Influencer Networks: Synchronized online personas who simulate consensus and credibility by mutually reinforcing aligned narratives.
  • Mil-Bloggers: Nationalist or veteran commentators who appear independent but validate regime goals while masking them as grassroots critique.
  • Organized Criminal Activity: Embedded criminal groups that execute influence functions covertly, often serving as proxies for state operations.
  • Social Media Manipulation: Botnets, trolls, and algorithm gaming used to fabricate engagement, suppress dissent, and engineer virality.
  • State-Aligned Social Groups: Foreign-facing activist-style organizations that co-opt local grievances to disseminate regime-aligned narratives under the guise of solidarity.
  • State-Content Aggregators: Alternative media platforms that normalize and embed state propaganda within broader ideological media diets.
  • State-Media Partnerships: Formal collaborations between authoritarian regimes and foreign media outlets to lend legitimacy to state narratives.
  • Train-the-Trainers Programs: Long-term influence structures that cultivate local actors to propagate aligned messaging organically within their communities.

These multipliers work horizontally (spreading messages across networks) and vertically (embedding them into trusted institutions or systems). Their power lies in architecture, not authorship, making influence campaigns harder to detect, challenge, or reverse. They form the backbone of modern information warfare by turning isolated signals into full-spectrum cognitive environments.

Mechanisms

Operational Multipliers work horizontally and vertically across digital, institutional, and commercial environments. Browse the mechanisms below:

Title Description
Campaign Synchronization Campaign synchronization involves the strategic coordination of influence operations across various domains to create overwhelming psychological effects on target audiences.
Celebrity Networks State actors strategically amplify and cross-reference targeted content across various platforms to create a facade of legitimacy and expand reach, thereby embedding manipulated narratives within information ecosystems.
Commercial Malign Influence Commercial entities now act as digital mercenaries, offering paid information manipulation services that detach influence from ideology and enable clients to outsource propaganda and disinformation campaigns.
Data Poisoning and Grooming Data poisoning and grooming involve the intentional creation and dissemination of customized content to influence AI training datasets, embedding specific biases and narratives into machine learning outputs.
Exploit Crises Influence operations strategically exploit crises by timing their messaging to coincide with emergencies, which enhances message penetration and accelerates narrative adoption due to heightened emotional vulnerability and reduced critical scrutiny among populations.
Inauthentic Accounts or Engagement Inauthentic accounts and engagement involve coordinated networks that artificially amplify social proof through rapid interactions, exploiting human psychology and platform algorithms to create the illusion of popularity and credibility.
Localize Content Localization involves adapting foreign messaging to seem domestically produced by translating and culturally tailoring it to align with local contexts, enhancing its credibility and shareability while masking its external origins.
Mil-Bloggers Mil-bloggers are seemingly independent military commentators who, while appearing grassroots, actually serve as controlled outlets for state narratives, enhancing credibility and shaping public perception while suppressing genuine dissent.
Online Influencer Coordination Online Influencer Coordination involves networks of influencers and algorithmically linked accounts that blend authentic engagement with covert promotion to manipulate public perception and discourse around divisive issues.
Platform Manipulation Algorithmic manipulation on digital platforms involves coordinated actions to artificially influence the visibility of information, shaping public perception by amplifying preferred narratives and suppressing opposing ones.
Social Media Amplification Coordinated networks of fake online personas amplify specific narratives on social media to create artificial consensus and manipulate public perception.
State-Aligned Organizing State-aligned social groups create the illusion of grassroots activism to promote state interests by organizing public demonstrations, silencing dissent, and spreading strategic narratives.
State-Content Syndicators State-Content Syndicators are entities that collect and redistribute state-controlled content, either by remixing it with unrelated information or by directly republishing it, thereby obscuring its origin and enhancing its reach.
State-Integrated Criminal Networks State-integrated criminal networks are hybrid entities that combine organized crime with political and intelligence systems to further regime goals through coercion, corruption, and illicit finance, acting as a shadow governance structure.
Train-the-Trainers State actors utilize trained intermediaries to embed within communities, promoting state-aligned narratives through trusted social channels, thereby creating a multiplier effect that enhances influence while maintaining plausible deniability.
