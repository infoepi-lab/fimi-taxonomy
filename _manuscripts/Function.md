# Function

## Function Overview

Function is not the surface description of an act but the role it plays
inside the machinery of influence. Most frameworks will stop at the
label—“cyberattack,” “press conference,” “public protest”—and maybe
speculate about intent. Function pushes deeper, asking where this thing
sits in the structure. Is it tightening control over decision-making?
Extending power into new territory? Masking the source of a narrative?
The same structural position can be filled by wildly different tactics:
an assassination and a treaty clause can both consolidate control; a
youth festival and a fake NGO can both manufacture legitimacy.

Legality, visibility, and even operational success are beside the point
here. A failed cyber intrusion can still function as a demonstration of
capability. A broadcast nobody saw might still validate a narrative for
its intended diplomatic audience. When you look through this lens, the
noise fades away, and the architecture begins to reveal itself.

The Infomap framework operates through eight recurring functions:
command and control, projection abroad, legitimation, obfuscation,
sabotage, operational multiplication, spillover, and structural terrain
shaping. They are less about the tools used and more about the strategic
job being done. A single campaign might involve stacking several
elements, pairing covert financing with media partnerships and legal
maneuvers, or running a sabotage operation under the cover of a
legitimacy play and seeing that layering is the point.

Function analysis enables the comparison of cases that would otherwise
not be in the same room, tracing the connective tissue between a
mercenary deployment in Africa and a disinformation push in a foreign
election, and anticipating moves before they occur. Once you can name
the role something plays, you can look for the other things that role
tends to require, and for the conditions that make it possible. It’s
less about tracking every move on the board and more about learning the
rules of the game.

### [**Command and Control**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1X1Z37w8kc_XQ_9GWRq5XH46vOHNJiLGI9dTqy2x-Twg/edit)

Defines who decides, who acts, and whose account of reality prevails. It
can be enforced physically — as Wagner enforces elite priorities without
formal state insignia — or cognitively, by removing interpreters of
events who are not already aligned, such as scientists, journalists, and
clergy. It can hide inside a treaty clause that supplies legal pretext
for intervention, as with the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan, or
manifest in a direct erasure of rival leadership, as in the Spetsnaz
assassination of Amin. State and oligarchic media alike perform the
quieter work of delimiting credibility; military power, overt or covert,
makes the boundaries real.

### [**Projection Abroad**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1CmX-uHMXc98v37jQLtTLFr_rbvcuXyWB3jeX7QlPkrM/edit)

Extends domestic capacity into foreign terrain. This may be diaspora
mobilization, state-aligned “aid,” cyber units like Fancy Bear or
Killnet, religious and cultural channels cultivating soft power, or
binding foreign elites through nuclear and infrastructure dependencies.
The goal is consistent: reproduce the home system’s control, legitimacy,
and narrative dominance beyond its borders.

[**Legitimation**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/10_-KJAJPb9MDIRXbItSWTeQjobnMTYOrZPIbOwvMaBs/edit)

Creates the appearance of credibility or consent. Cultural programs —
such as youth festivals, “peace” councils, and sporting events —
simulate voluntary alignment. State-backed activism via front groups
produces the illusion of external endorsement. Awards, philanthropy, and
curated academic access bind prestige to regime narratives.
Pseudo-experts, fabricated institutions, citation loops, and mimicry of
scientific or activist discourse make state messaging indistinguishable
from independent civil output.

### [**Obfuscation**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1n9CpyfH-nnujB8LIfZOF_8Pynj6auIxPm4HrJ4rsYxw/edit)

Masks origin, affiliation, or purpose. Covert media fronts, shell
organizations, and paid influencers without disclosure present as
independent while repeating sanctioned lines. Soviet-era “friendship
societies” or contemporary entities like InfoRos and Rossotrudnichestvo
allow the same pattern: strategic effect with plausible deniability.

### [**Sabotage**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1zkf3eU8vV4sWRY6OPfpEQCj-bbbtejA38m0MUr-5e4w/edit)

Does not aim to persuade; it aims to degrade. Corruption export,
strategic lawfare, covert criminal activity, demoralization campaigns,
falsified history, and manufactured extremism all fragment trust and
institutional capacity. Often invisible, the effect is cumulative:
systemic resilience eroded from within.

### [**Operational Multiplier**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1ZDiapuspJLk5Yi8tWDJ8_v3Ys-oUD4BWHSr6FuzSAnQ/edit)

Magnifies the reach and persistence of other functions. Local media
partnerships confer borrowed credibility; PR firms, platforms, and
algorithmic manipulation shift the field under a “neutral” banner.
Citation ecosystems extend narrative lifespan. Trolls, influencers, and
“independent” bloggers carry messages farther without exposing
coordination. Train-the-trainer models and allied movements embed
messaging so it can propagate without direct management.

### [**Spillover**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1EI1L7sTErVOiwGVYNKyojiZW-2T-L6wxX07Du_J9fnU/edit)

The unplanned expansion of influence. Foreign media cite state sources;
wire services syndicate stories; unaffiliated actors with compatible
aims carry the same narratives. There is no direct orchestration, but
the state can tolerate—even value—the free extension of its footprint.

### [**Structural Terrain**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/1/d/1L9boVBLYHUrOCT8TvMQiaOIujLwM6xSIfIcV1P1RcRA/edit)

The persistent conditions in which all other functions operate: legal
regimes, infrastructure, demographics, technological access, and
cultural baselines. These are not actions but substrates. A fragmented
media environment, lax regulation, or low scientific literacy creates
exploitable vulnerabilities; greater resilience in these domains can
blunt even sophisticated operations.

Function analysis strips away surface variation to expose systemic role.
It is how you separate noise from architecture, and tactics from the
intent that structures them.
